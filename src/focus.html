How to read these numbers
Wallets, not people

Counts represent unique wallet addresses. A person may control more than one wallet. A wallet can appear in both migration counters.

Migrated Pi, not current balance

Totals combine recorded lockups for confirmed events in the stated period. Claims and spending do not reduce these totals.

Received, then classified

A receipt groups lockups for one wallet and transaction. Pending receipts may appear in the Top 20, but enter confirmed totals only after verification.

Migration numbers are established from available on-chain evidence. They do not reveal a user's identity, mining-app balance or eligibility. The overview accumulates since tracking started; only the Top 20 uses a rolling seven-day window.
